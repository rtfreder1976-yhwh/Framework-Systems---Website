Common questions

A few things plumbing owners usually ask first.

Do I need to replace everything I already use?

Not necessarily. The first job is to identify the leak. Sometimes that means a cleaner system inside what you already have. Sometimes it means a tighter front-desk layer around it.

Can we start with only missed-call recovery?

Yes. In many shops that is the cleanest first wedge. It is simple to understand, easy to test, and often the fastest place to prove value.

Do you only work with local plumbing companies?

No. The offer is built for plumbing companies wherever the operating fit is right. The site is plumbing-specific, but the work is not limited to one local market.

How fast can this go live?

A reliable first version is usually measured in weeks, not months. The exact timing depends on the current account state, phone setup, and how much needs to be cleaned up first.

Is this just AI voice in disguise?

No. AI can be part of the system, especially for after-hours or overflow, but the core offer is revenue recovery and conversion improvement. The technology is there to support that, not replace the strategy.

What if we are not a fit?

Then we will say so. Some companies need lead generation first. Others need internal process cleanup before more automation helps. We would rather be direct than sell the wrong thing.
