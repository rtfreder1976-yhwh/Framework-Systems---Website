Before you reach out

Questions we hear most often.

Do I need to know anything about automation or CRMs before the call?

Not at all. Most of our clients have never heard of Framework CRM or Framework Engine before we met. The call is designed for operators, not technologists. You just describe how your business runs — we handle the rest.

How quickly can you actually get things running?

Most Blueprint tier clients are fully live within two weeks of the discovery call. Build and Architect typically run two to three weeks depending on integration complexity. We don't hand it off until it works.

What if I'm not sure which tier is right for me?

That's exactly what the discovery call figures out. We'll map your operation, identify the highest-leverage gaps, and tell you which tier addresses them directly — and which one doesn't. You won't leave the call guessing.

Do you work with businesses outside the Southeast?

We're based in Tuscumbia, Alabama and our roots are in the Southeast — but the Framework OS runs remotely. If you're a service business owner who found us and the work sounds right, reach out. We'll have an honest conversation about fit.

What happens after the system is built?

Your monthly retainer covers ongoing monitoring, updates when your business changes, priority support, and a quarterly strategy review. We stay in the system with you — we're not building and walking away.

Are there long-term contracts?

We work month-to-month after the setup is complete. We'd rather earn your continued business by delivering results than lock you in with a contract. That said, the systems we build are designed to become a permanent part of how your business runs.
