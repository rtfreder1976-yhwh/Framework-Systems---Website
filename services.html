Clarity — explained

Most dashboards tell you what already happened. Yours will tell you what's coming.

Service owners hate siloed data. That's why Clarity combines your lead data, job data, and financial data into one cross-referenced dashboard. See exactly which technician is driving the most profitable jobs. Track which lead sources actually convert to revenue. Know your true customer lifetime value.

There are two kinds of numbers in every business. Lag measures are outcomes — revenue earned, reviews received, clients won. They tell you the final score. You can track them, but by the time you see a problem, the week is already over.

Lead measures are the actions that drive those outcomes — quotes sent within two hours, missed calls recovered the same day, follow-ups completed per lead. These you control right now. When you move a lead measure, the lag measure follows.

Clarity pulls from multiple sources — your CRM, calendar, Stripe, QuickBooks, Google Reviews — and unifies everything into one intelligent view. Every Monday morning, you see not just what happened — but whether you're on track to hit your targets before the month ends. That's the difference between managing your business and reacting to it.

Rooted in

The lead and lag measure philosophy comes from Traction by Gino Wickman and the 4 Disciplines of Execution — the same operating framework we run The Valley Clean Team on today.
