SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective date: October 3, 2026

These SMS Terms and Conditions apply to text message programs operated by VALLEY SERVICES COMPANY LLC, doing business as Framework Systems ("Framework Systems," "we," "us," or "our"), on behalf of participating local businesses. By joining a program or texting with us, you agree to these terms.

1. Program Descriptions

Loyalty and Rewards Program

Participating businesses offer a loyalty and rewards program by text message. You join by checking in at the business using an in-store QR code, which opens a web form. On that form, you enter your mobile number and check an unchecked consent box to agree to receive texts. Once enrolled, you may receive recurring automated marketing and loyalty text messages from that business, including:

  • Points updates after you check in
  • Notices when you unlock a reward
  • Birthday offers
  • Offers inviting you back if you have not visited in a while

Missed-Call Text-Back

When you call a participating business and your call is missed, you may receive one text message from that business's phone number so it can follow up with you about your call. This service starts only when you call the business. It does not enroll you in any marketing program. If you reply, the business may text you back to continue the conversation you started.

2. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. For the loyalty and rewards program, the number of messages depends on how often you visit and on the offers the business runs. For missed-call text-back, you will receive one text per missed call, plus any replies to messages you send.

3. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

4. How to Opt Out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from that business's program. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again. You may also reply END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or QUIT.

5. How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any message for help. You can also contact us at todd@frameworksystems.co or 256-824-9084.

6. Consent Not Required for Purchase

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase. You do not have to join any text message program to buy goods or services from any participating business.

7. Carrier Disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to your mobile carrier's network and coverage.

8. Eligibility and Your Number

You must be the account holder or authorized user of the mobile number you provide. If you change or give up your mobile number, please reply STOP from that number first, or contact us, so messages are not sent to the new owner.

9. Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. Please review our Privacy Policy to learn how we collect, use, and protect your information. We do not share your mobile information or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
