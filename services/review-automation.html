The math

Reviews don't measure who's best.
They measure who asks.

The overwhelming majority of consumers read reviews before choosing a service provider. They don't call the best company. They call the company with the most recent, positive reviews. The difference between you and the competitor with 3x your review count isn't quality. It's whether you have a system that asks after every job. They do. You don't. That gap is deciding who gets the next call.

Without the system

What happens after a job today

You finish the work. The customer thanks you. You leave. Maybe you remember to ask for a review. Maybe you don't. They never leave one.

  • Reviews only come from the rare customer who self-motivates
  • Bad experiences get reviewed more than good ones
  • Your competitor with average work has 3x your reviews
  • Every new customer Googles you — and sees the gap
