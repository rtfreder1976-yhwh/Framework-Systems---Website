The math

Let's do the math on what voicemail
is actually costing you.

Say you get 40 calls a week. Miss 27% of them — that's industry average. That's 11 missed calls. 85% of those callers won't try again. At $450 per average job, that's $4,207 a week walking out the door. Over a year? $218,000. Not because you lost a marketing battle. Because nobody texted back.
