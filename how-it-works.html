The Discovery Call.
One question. No pitch.

We start with one question: what's the most annoying part of running your business? That's it. You talk. We listen. We write down what you say — because your words become the foundation of everything we build.

This isn't a sales call dressed up as a consultation. No slide deck. No feature walkthrough. We're trying to understand your actual operation — where it works, where it breaks, and what fixing the right things would mean for your bottom line and your sanity.

Framework Clarity — how it works

Most dashboards tell you what already happened. Yours will tell you what's coming.

There are two kinds of numbers in every business. Lag measures are outcomes — revenue earned, reviews received, clients won. They tell you the score after the game is over. You can track them, but you can't change them once they land.

Lead measures are the actions that drive those outcomes — quotes sent within two hours, missed calls recovered the same day, follow-ups completed per lead. These you can control right now. And when you move a lead measure, the lag measure follows.

Framework Clarity is built around both. Every week you see not just what happened — but whether you're on track to hit what you want next week, next month, and next quarter. That's the difference between managing your business and reacting to it.

Rooted in

The lead and lag measure framework comes from Traction by Gino Wickman and the 4 Disciplines of Execution — the same operating system we use to run The Valley Clean Team.
