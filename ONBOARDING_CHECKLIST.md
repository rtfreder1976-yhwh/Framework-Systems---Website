# Internal Onboarding Checklist: Google Presence Starter (Realtor)

Use this checklist to ensure every new Realtor client is properly set up for the **Google Presence Starter ($100/mo)**.

## 1. Initial Setup & Access
- [ ] **Verify Payment**: Confirm the $100/mo subscription is active.
- [ ] **GBP Manager Access**: Verify that `todd@frameworksystems.co` has been added as a Manager to their Google Business Profile.
- [ ] **Onboarding Form Review**: Review the client's submitted `onboarding.html` data for:
    - Primary service area.
    - Top 3 specialties.
    - Preferred Call-to-Action (CTA).
    - Review request preference.

## 2. Google Business Profile Audit & Cleanup
- [ ] **NAP Consistency**: Ensure Name, Address, and Phone number match their professional license and website.
- [ ] **Category Optimization**: Verify the primary category is "Real Estate Agency" or "Real Estate Consultant" as appropriate.
- [ ] **Service List Update**: Add the top 3 specialties identified in the onboarding form to the "Services" section.
- [ ] **Description Rewrite**: Craft a search-optimized business description (750 chars) emphasizing their farm area and specialties.

## 3. Conversion Optimization
- [ ] **CTA Implementation**: Update the "Website" or "Booking" link in GBP to point to the client's preferred destination.
- [ ] **Photo Audit**: Check current photos. If missing headshots or local area photos, request them via the shared folder.
- [ ] **Review Request Setup**: Configure the review request template (SMS or Email) based on client preference.

## 4. Delivery & Reporting
- [ ] **Initial Visibility Check**: Run a baseline search for their primary keywords in their farm area.
- [ ] **Intro Email**: Send a welcome email confirming setup is complete and providing the link to their shared photo folder.
- [ ] **Monthly Recurring Task**: Schedule the first "Monthly Visibility Check-in" for 30 days from today.

---
**Product Goal**: Clean up the foundation and generate early visibility wins to pave the way for an upgrade to the *Local Demand Builder ($399/mo)*.
