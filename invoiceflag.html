What it looks for

The line items that slip past a glance at the total.

Most fulfillment bills get paid on the total. InvoiceFlag reads every line and pulls out the ones worth a question before the money leaves.

Flag list
  • Duplicate tracking / accessorials.
  • Fees that were never on the contract.
  • DIM and DAS stacked on the same shipment.
  • Peak / residential / extended-area surprises.
  • Pick or order counts that don’t match the backup.
